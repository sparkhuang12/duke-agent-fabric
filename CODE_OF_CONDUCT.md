# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Debate technical decisions with evidence; do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or expose private information about another participant.

Project spaces are for improving Duke Agent Fabric. Spam, intentionally unsafe contributions, undisclosed generated payloads, credential sharing and attempts to move vulnerability details into public threads may be removed. Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community and its users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed with confidentiality proportional to the situation.

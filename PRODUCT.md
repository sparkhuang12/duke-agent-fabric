# Duke Agent Fabric

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Chinese-speaking staff prepare documents from organizational materials. A different authorized reviewer checks pending drafts and records feedback. Operators manage documents, tasks and audit records.

## Product Purpose

Support a manuscript from source materials through drafting, independent review and subsequent revisions. The author can return to submitted versions within the manuscript and see the decisions that shaped them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Six roles cover drafting, policy questions, document search, minutes, comparison and proofreading. Review permissions, scoped context and model availability come from the backend. The development operations dashboard depends on the optional legacy routes. Interface assets must load locally to support private deployments.

## Brand Commitments

Duke is the product name. The user chose the visual language of a youth magazine: bright contrasting colours, photographic covers and lively editorial layouts. Long-form Chinese text, administrative facts, status and review feedback must remain easy to read.
